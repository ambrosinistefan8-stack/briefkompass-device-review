About this English interfaceContent, privacy and limitations

The controls are in English. The fictional letter, its quoted passages and generated findings remain in German; the app does not translate them.

Try the rules-only demo with a fictional letter. The missing-document example includes a possible date and refers to a missing attachment. The unclear-information example mentions 14 days without a start date. All findings need checking against the original.
