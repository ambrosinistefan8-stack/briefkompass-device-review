English guideControls, privacy and limitations

BriefKompass is a German-language learning prototype for exploring synthetic letters. This guide explains the controls; the letter and results remain in German and are not translated by the app.

Try the rules-only demo

  1. Under Beispielbrief laden (synthetisch), choose a fictional sample: missing document, objection period, or unclear information.
  2. Leave DEMO selected and press Brief prüfen (check letter). This mode uses rules, not AI.
  3. Under Ergebnisse (results), inspect Anliegen (matters), Fristen (dates or time limits), and Offene Punkte (open points). Select Im Brief markieren to highlight a passage in the original.
  4. Open Vor der Aufgabenübernahme prüfen, check the passage and context against the original, and confirm both checks before selecting Als Aufgabe übernehmen. Then open Aufgaben (tasks). A date is not copied into the task; after checking the original, a person can enter one manually. Tasks stay in this browser; storage availability may vary.

Two fictional examples to compare

Nachweis fehlt: a missing document and a candidate date of 15 January 2027; the letter refers to an attachment that is not included.

Unklare Angaben: a relative period of 14 days; its start and end need checking, and no reference number is detected. The app does not calculate a due date.

Privacy, modes and limits

Use synthetic examples only. Review every finding against the original. This prototype gives no legal advice or official decision.

  • DEMO: rule-based; no AI model or letter transmission.
  • Lokal: device-only inference via the Firebase AI Logic SDK. The demo remains available if Chrome cannot run the model.
